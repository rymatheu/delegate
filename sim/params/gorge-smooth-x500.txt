# The smooth flight of the X500 in the gorge (sim/README.md "Gorge flight", "The smooth flight"): navigation and
# attitude-shaping parameters sent with PARAM_SET after the parameter download (sim/gorge_lockstep.py --params), on top
# of the X500 image (build/gorge-port/storage-x500.bin, sim/params/reference-x500.txt). Same route, same plant.
# Mechanism (ArduPilot 802db19de1, libraries/AP_Math/SplineCurve.cpp): along a spline leg the target speed is capped by
# the leg's curvature, speed_max = sqrt(0.5 x WP_ACC / curvature) (the vertical part of the cornering acceleration
# limited by 0.5 x WP_ACC_Z), and moves toward that cap at +-0.5 x WP_ACC with no jerk limit. With WP_SPD above the cap
# the vehicle accelerates and brakes on every leg (the fast flight). ArduPilot's spline legs leave each waypoint along
# W[k+1] - W[k-1] (about two legs long), so a leg's turning is packed into its middle: on this plan's short legs (5 and 8)
# the spline turns on radii of 2.3 and 3.2 m although the gorge's centerline bends no tighter than 20.6 m.
WP_SPD 4             # m/s (image 2): the fastest speed held within +-10 % on this route; above it the curvature caps of legs 5, 8, 10 bring back the dips
WP_ACC 5             # m/s^2 (default 2.5; @Range 0.5 5, the largest): sets both the curvature caps (0.5 x WP_ACC lateral) and the along-track slew
WP_ACC_Z 3           # m/s^2 (default 1): the vertical part of the spline's cornering limit; with 1 the floor's rise caps legs 10 and 11 at about 1.3 m/s
WP_JERK 5            # m/s^3 (default 1): time constant WP_ACC / WP_JERK = 1 s (default 5 s) of the track-progress scaler, so the start's lag recovers within leg 3
WP_YAW_BEHAVIOR 3    # face along the GPS course (default 2: the heading of the spline target's velocity, which copies the S-kinks)
ATC_INPUT_TC 0.15    # s (Copter default 0.10, "Medium" in the parameter's list): softer attitude input shaping of the bank and pitch commands
ATC_RATE_R_MAX 45    # deg/s (default 0, unlimited): roll rate limit of the attitude command model; the kinks of legs 4, 5, 7, 8 asked up to 69 deg/s
ATC_RATE_P_MAX 45    # deg/s (default 0, unlimited): the same for pitch
