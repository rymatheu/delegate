## Mon Oct 26 19:03:22 EDT 2026 -- attempt 1: the candidate in the brief, checked against the literal constraints
Finding F1 (D3 as stated is unsatisfiable in the interesting case). A2 and G2 do not mention U, so A2 cap G2 is U-independent and A cap A2 cap G2 sub G is equivalent to A2 cap G2 sub forall_U(not A cup G). For a fence that depends on u (square of half-side s, s ranging over (0,inf)) forall_U(not A cup G) only admits target 0 at every tick, so G2 would have to pin the target to the origin for every valid mission. The weakened form G2 cap A2 sub G (used in the Lean delegation theorem) is even stronger. Conclusion: the relation between u and y can only be carried by G1, so D3 must keep G1. Fix: D3+ := A cap G1 cap A2 cap G2 sub G. Composition then follows from D1, D2, D3+ (rule_general followed by tightening), still with the literal D2 (G1 sub A2).

Finding F2 (D2 as stated kills environment conditions in A2). If A2 = V(w) and P(w,e) (dt range, parameter ranges), G1 sub A2 means G1 sub forall_E(V cap P) = V cap forall_E P, and forall_E P is empty whenever P constrains dt. So G1 is empty. Fix: give M2 the contract (V, not P cup T) instead of (V cap P, T). Same implementers: M2 cap V sub (not P cup T) iff M2 cap V cap P sub T. The environment part P must then be delivered by the top-level A; this shows up inside the residual as the requirement that A(u,.) entails P(w,.).

## Mon Oct 26 19:11:28 EDT 2026 -- attack A2 (decidability of the projection; pointwise reduction)
Claim: for phi = beta and Box alpha and Dia gamma_1..Dia gamma_m (full window [0,H], gamma_0 := true), forall signals (phi -> Box c) iff not beta or some alpha cap gamma_j empty or alpha sub c; forall signals (phi -> Dia d) iff not beta or some alpha cap gamma_j sub d; provided H >= m. Brute force over all signals on a 4-point tick domain, random sets: 3056 cases with H>=m, 0 disagreements. With H<m: 95 disagreements in 944 cases; with only H+1>=m: 25 disagreements. So the side condition H >= m is necessary and is kept in the statement. Script: notes/algorithm/checks/pointwise.py.
General case (operators with other windows, until): unroll and run real QE over all samples of E,Y; decidable (Tarski-Seidenberg) but doubly exponential in (H+1)(|E|+|Y|); never touches code.
Fragment of the output: QE result is quantifier-free real arithmetic over the static samples of U and W, i.e. a PolySTL formula without temporal operators read at tick 0. If U were per-tick, an output atom may couple two ticks, which is outside PolySTL (recorded in the write-up).

## Mon Oct 26 19:14:18 EDT 2026 -- attacks A3..A9
A3 (trivial delegates). Can the output be (T,F)/(F,T)-like? A1 is empty only if A is empty (vacuous query; now reported explicitly in step 5). If V is empty or T_j* is everything, R forces G on all targets, so G1 serves no input and Bad = A1 is reported instead of success. G1 is built from A,G,V,P,T only (fixed tube shape, size independent of the code), so it is never the query with M2 inlined. Outcome: holds; added the vacuous-A report.
A4 (hidden wire). A, G are over Pi\W, so F4+F3 give exists_W(M1 cap M2) |= (A,G) from M1 cap M2 |= (A,G): hiding the wire is sound (Theorem c). Code-level hidden semantics of W (from controller-ports.md): lat=lng=0 and alt=0 mean 'use default', unknown item ids are skipped, arcs/splines/speed-change items alter the path. If V admitted them the polyline through the listed waypoints is not what is flown and step 2 fails to certify T; so V must exclude them (read from the guards). Outcome: handled by steps 1-2, V text names sentinel exclusion.
A5 (A mentioning outputs, e.g. a tracking assumption relating the estimate in E to the target in Y). Step 3 uses exists_{E cup Y} A, step 4 forall_{E cup Y}; no proof step uses that A avoids Y. Outcome: algorithm and proofs unchanged; only the usual caveat that such an A can be falsified by the controller itself.
A6 (G2 mentioning U). M2 cap A2 is over Pi\U, so by F3 M2 cap A2 sub G2 iff M2 cap A2 sub forall_U G2: any U-dependence in G2 can be dropped to forall_U G2 without changing what M2 must do, and the alphabet requirement holds by construction since chain members only use W,E,Y. Outcome: holds.
A7 (shared environment inputs). Two places: D2 with P in A2 empties G1 (F2 in log) -> P moved to G2; inside R the branch not P requires A(u,.) sub P(w,.) at every (u,w) the planner uses. Example: a start disc of radius 1 and minimum leg 5 forces |w1-h| >= 6. Refinement: P may take over the conjuncts of A that mention only E (A is inside them, so the branch is vacuous for them and the controller check becomes narrower). Outcome: holds; refinement added to step 1.
A8 (quantifier over Y). Replacing forall y by exists y (tempting when M2 is deterministic) is unsound: the tube contains targets inside and outside the fence and the code may produce the outside one. With forall y over the over-approximation T, G1 is weakest relative to G2, not to M2 (corner-cut example (0,-49)). Outcome: forall kept; conservatism stated in the write-up; repair only by a thinner certified tube.
A9 (stadium atom). Polynomial disjunction for 'within rho of [a,b]' vs exact segment distance on 200000 random cases: 0 disagreements (notes/algorithm/checks/stadium.py).

## Mon Oct 26 19:15:14 EDT 2026 -- attacks A10..A16
A10 (horizon mismatch). If T is certified on [0,H2] with H2<H while G has Box over [0,H], the targets after H2 are unconstrained, so R is empty wherever A is satisfiable and step 5 reports Bad = A1. Fix kept in step 2: certify on the horizon of A and G. The reach part of T can only be certified if V bounds the mission length so it finishes inside H; that is checked by step 2, not assumed.
A11 (nondeterministic dt). (a) Runs of the code at nominal dt only bound rho from below; they never certify. (b) Seconds deadlines: tick window [0, floor(D/dtmax)] is sound (no tick longer than dtmax) and costs the factor dtmax/dtmin; with the code's own range (1/2000..1/50) that is 40x and the reach guarantee is hopeless, with A's range (0.0024..0.0026) it is 1.08. So P should take over A's E-only conjuncts (A7 refinement). Added one sentence to section 5.
A12 (cost vs monolithic, example sizes). Monolithic BMC: code of M1 and M2 unrolled over ~1.25e4 ticks with u, e free, repeated per planner revision. Here: step 2 = the M2 unrolling alone, O(log k) times, once; steps 3-5 in the pointwise class eliminate 5 reals (dt, y in R^2, p0 in R^2) and in the example need no solver at all; each planner check is against a quantifier-free formula over 9 static reals (s,h,g,w1,w2) with no signals. Outcome: argued by encoding size, not by a worst-case complexity theorem; stated so in the write-up.
A13 (the brief's 'A1 := A restricted to U'). Any superset of exists_E A satisfies D1; a syntactic restriction that drops E-conjuncts is such a superset, while anything smaller breaks D1. On inputs outside exists_E A, R is everything, so a larger A1 does not enlarge Bad as long as V is nonempty. Outcome: exists_{E cup Y} A kept as the exact choice; syntactic restriction acceptable.
A14 (the brief's 'tighten A2 to the W-projection of A cap G1'). D2 needs G1 sub A2; G1 contains every valid mission for inputs outside A1 (vacuous part), so exists_U(G1) = V and tightening A2 to exists(A cap G1) breaks D2. Correct variant: replace G1 by G1 cap A1 (same planner obligation, since M1 cap A1 sub G1 iff M1 cap A1 sub G1 cap A1) and then A2 := V cap exists_U(G1 cap A1); D3+ survives because the new sets are inside the old, and M2's obligation only gets weaker (assumption strengthened). Optional, not needed for correctness; kept out of the two pages.
A15 (deterministic planner). (f) builds a possibly nondeterministic M1 := G1 cup not A1. When Bad is empty a deterministic planner also exists: G1 is semi-algebraic, so a semi-algebraic choice function w = f(u) exists (definable choice over real closed fields). Outcome: realizability statement unchanged.
A16 (binary search on the chain). If M2 cap V cap P sub T_j and T_j sub T_j' then also sub T_j', so the certified indices are an up-set and binary search returns the least one. Monotonicity of R in T (Theorem e) makes it the best certified choice.
