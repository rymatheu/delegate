# machine, failsafes, health and arming checks, home position. A task module: `commander start`
# spawns a task (px4_task_spawn_cmd) whose loop runs every 10 ms (px4_usleep(10 ms),
# COMMANDER_MONITORING_INTERVAL), or at once again while vehicle_command has unread messages.
# Read by px4/ports.py and px4/build.sh. Paths relative to $PX4_ROOT.
